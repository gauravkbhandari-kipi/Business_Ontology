The Challenge & The Answer

"Net production" should mean one thing, everywhere

The same term can mean different things in different teams. kipi.ai worked with ConocoPhillips data stewards to put canonical definitions directly in Snowflake. They sit next to the data, are governed by the same RBAC, and are used by Cortex Analyst and Cortex Agents.

Core Model

An oil and gas knowledge graph

Domain

Upstream, Midstream, Land, HSE, Drilling, Production Operations, Commercial. A domain is the smallest unit you can grant access on.

Node TERM

Well, Lease, Reservoir, NRI, WI, LOE, Netback, Emissions Event, typed as METRIC, ENTITY, ATTRIBUTE or CONCEPT.

Relationship

Well PART_OF Field · Daily Production DERIVED_FROM Meter Reading · Facility DEPENDS_ON Pipeline.

Representation ASSOCIATION

Links each concept to the production table, well master view, reserves Semantic View or Search Service that implements it.

Governance Principles

Every change needs steward approval

Nothing AI-generated becomes active without human confirmation. An edit to a live definition opens an edit card, and the live version stays active until the edit is approved.

Steward accountability

Domain stewards own the accuracy of their definitions.

Evidence-grounded

Definitions come from allocations, reserves documentation and regulatory rules. The AUM principle: nothing fabricated goes active.

Domain-scoped

Each node belongs to exactly one domain. Domains are never invented.

Least privilege

READ for consumers, SUGGEST for proposers, MODIFY for approvers.

Security & Access Control

Ontology domains are securable objects

Uses your existing ConocoPhillips SSO and MFA session with no separate login. Every SYSTEM$ call runs under the active role. A lookup you aren't allowed to make returns not-found, not access-denied.

Proof of Concept · Delivered by kipi.ai

  
A small POC: Production Operations domain

  
A tightly scoped POC: one domain, a few existing Semantic Views, three steward roles, and one Cortex Agent answering production questions from governed definitions.

  

    

      

      
Scope & Discovery kipiCOP
Pick the Production Operations domain, its priority KPIs (Daily Production, LOE, Netback, NRI) and the source Semantic Views.

      
Enable & Secure Snowflakekipi
Confirm the feature flag with GET_GLOSSARY_SUMMARY(), create the domain, and grant READ, SUGGEST and MODIFY roles.

      
Bootstrap the Ontology kipi
Ingest the production Semantic Views and enrich them with Cortex Sense. Every item is stamped SV_DEFINITION or CORTEX_SENSE.

      
Steward Review COP
Production Ops stewards check each draft against allocation rules and approve or reject it. Nothing AI-generated goes live without a steward.

      
Link & Relate kipiCOP
Add representations to tables and columns, add relationships (Well PART_OF Field, Daily Production DERIVED_FROM Meter Reading), and write comments back.

      
Ontology-grounded Agent kipi
Build a Cortex Agent that uses ontology search and the linked Semantic Views.

      
Validate & Handover COPkipi
Review the audit trail with GET_GLOSSARY_TERM_HISTORY, report findings, and plan rollout to Land, HSE and Reserves.

Observability & Audit

  
Full traceability, from concept to change

  
Every node carries a provenance stamp. The first write sets it and later approvals don't change it, so ConocoPhillips can always audit what came from people and what came from AI.

Limitations & Availability

  
Know the current boundaries

  

    
!
Private Preview
Must be enabled per account by the Snowflake account team. SUGGEST and edit cards may not be available everywhere.

    
!
Domain-level stewardship only
You can't assign a steward to an individual node. Use subdomains for finer control.

    
!
No rename or move APIs
Renaming a domain or moving a node means recreating it, which breaks audit history.

    
!
No ACCOUNT_USAGE view
Auditing relies on SYSTEM$ functions. SHOW GRANTS ON ONTOLOGY DOMAIN is not supported.

    
!
One-way sync
Imports from dbt and BI tools are one-way. Comment egress is a skill-orchestrated workaround.

    
!
Batch-approval risk
Always scope APPROVE_ALL_* calls by session or domain.
